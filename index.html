How the board works

The board is the public surface. Nothing is invented here in advance of a real post.

Read the full note

  1. A problem is posted

    A mathematics or physics problem occupies its track slot on the board. The statement appears only when it is actually posted.

  2. It stays current for that track

    Each track holds one current problem. Replacing it moves the previous problem toward the archive.

  3. A writeup later, if it earns one

    Writeups are not automatic. If a problem earns one, the writeup is published after the problem, not in place of it.

Archive

Posted problems, and any writeups they earn, will be collected here. The archive is empty.
