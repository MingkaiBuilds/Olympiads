Method

How the board works

Olympiads is a public board for IMO and IPhO style problem-solving. Daily puzzles live here. The site is the board, the tracks, and the archive — not a solutions dump, and not a place that invents problems in order to look finished.

  1. A problem is posted

    A problem occupies the mathematics slot or the physics slot. The statement appears on the board only when it is actually posted. Empty slots stay empty.

  2. It is the current problem for that track

    Each track holds one current problem. The same slot is shown on the daily board and on the track page. Replacing it sends the previous problem toward the archive.

  3. A writeup later, if it earns one

    A writeup is not owed to every post. If a problem earns one — because the work is worth keeping — it is published after the problem, not instead of it, and not in advance.

  4. The archive keeps the record

    Posted problems, and any writeups they earn, collect in the archive by date and track. The 20 August 2026 posts are IMO Puzzle 1 and IPhO Puzzle 1. Only the IPhO problem has a writeup.

What this site is

This is Mikail Stewart’s olympiad site — also Mingkai, 明凯. It is for International Mathematical Olympiad and International Physics Olympiad problem-solving done in public. It is not the personal hub, not the autonoetics memory site, not Frontiers, and not Thinking.

What it is not

  • Not a catalogue of invented sample problems.
  • Not an answer key, and not a promise that every problem gets a writeup.
  • Not a leak of live contest papers.
