What this track holds

The International Mathematical Olympiad is a proof contest. Work on this track is meant to sit in that tradition: a statement, time spent on it, and — if the problem earns one — a writeup after the fact.

IMO papers are classically built from algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory. Those are the areas this track is oriented toward. They are not a list of problems, and they are not a promise that every area will appear.

The first mathematics problem on this site is the one posted to the board: IMO Puzzle 1. No writeup has been published for it.
