What this track holds

The International Physics Olympiad is a theoretical and experimental contest. Work on this track is meant to sit in that tradition: a statement, time spent on it, and — if the problem earns one — a writeup after the fact.

IPhO theory papers classically draw on mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics and statistical physics, oscillations and waves, and modern physics. Those are the areas this track is oriented toward. They are not a list of problems, and they are not a promise that every area will appear.

The first physics problem on this site is the one posted to the board: IPhO Puzzle 1. This page does not invent further statements.
